Wholesale Manufacturing

Custom Corporate Uniforms, Manufactured to Order

Cotton Haven is a wholesale uniform manufacturer. We do not sell individual items — we partner with organisations, retailers and uniform programs to produce complete corporate uniform ranges, built exactly to your specifications.

Tell us the garment types you need and select your preferred fabric. We handle everything from pattern development and sampling through to bulk production and delivery.

Corporate Garments We Manufacture
Structured corporate blazers and suit jackets in woven poly viscose, poly wool or stretch blends. Single and double-breasted styles, fully lined, available in bespoke colour and button options.
Flat-front and pleated dress trousers in wrinkle-resistant woven fabrics with optional stretch. Colour matched to your full uniform suite.
Tailored corporate shorts in smart casual and business casual styles. Wrinkle-resistant fabrics, available colour matched to your full uniform suite.
Dress shirts in cotton poplin, oxford weave or poly cotton blends. Slim, regular and relaxed fits with spread, button-down or mandarin collar options and logo placement.
Women's corporate blouses and tailored tops in soft woven fabrics. Various necklines, sleeve lengths and tuck or relaxed silhouettes to complement the full uniform range.
Pencil, A-line and pleated skirt styles in suiting-weight woven fabrics. Colour matched to jackets and trousers for a coordinated corporate look.
Corporate shift dresses and tailored shirt dresses in woven fabrics with stretch options for ease of movement in professional environments.
Fine-gauge knit cardigans in jersey or interlock fabrics. A versatile layering piece that complements both formal and smart-casual corporate uniforms.
Corporate crew-neck and V-neck knit jumpers in fleece, French terry or fine-gauge jersey. Ideal for cooler environments or as a branded mid-layer over a shirt.
Knit and woven corporate vests — from tailored waistcoats to performance softshell styles. Lightweight branding options including embroidery and woven labels.
Classic and modern-fit corporate polo shirts in piqué knit or performance mesh. Available in 30+ colours with embroidered or heat-transfer logo options.
